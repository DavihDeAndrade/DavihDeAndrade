## About Me

I am a FullStack Developer and Automation/Electronics Specialist at SENAI CIMATEC, Brazil. My work combines software engineering, IoT systems, and cloud infrastructure. I specialize in backend development, designing and building robust, scalable services end-to-end, while also being capable across the full stack, from embedded hardware logic to web and mobile interfaces to data science and machine learning to robotics. I also have solid experience in Linux administration, managing systems, configuring environments, and maintaining infrastructure.

My focus areas include:

- Distributed backend systems and REST/event-driven APIs
- OOP design patterns and clean, maintainable architecture
- IoT pipelines, industrial automation and embedded integrations
- Cloud-native architectures

I have been working with research in Software Engineering fields for 9 years and i have been formally working with P&DI for 5 years. Every developed project is a new world with a different objective and new technologies to learn. A few areas i have already worked with:

- Backend and frontend development (on-permise and cloud-native)
- Mobile development
- Embedded systems
- Distributed and parallel systems
- Industrial PLC programming
- Industrial, collaborative and medical robotics
- Computer vision

Also, i am currently pursuing a degree in Computer Engineering, with an expected graduation in the second half of 2027.
